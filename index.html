Pose-posterior grasp planning

Robot grasps planned on where an object could be, not on one pose estimate.

A pose that passes the accuracy test can still drop the object. Planning on a pose posterior lifts it.

Top-1 grasp success 0.791 to 0.859, level with the annotated pose (0.856).
